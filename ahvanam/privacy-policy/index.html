Privacy Policy for Ahvanam

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.Ahvanam

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Ahvanam mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal information.

Your invitation details stay on your device. To create invitation cards for weddings, functions and other events, you may enter details such as names (of the couple, family members or hosts), the event date and time, the venue name and address, and personal messages. This information is used and stored only on your device to create your cards. It is never uploaded to our servers or sent to us or any third party, and we cannot see it.

Photos: You can add photos to your cards by choosing them from your gallery or taking them with your camera. The App accesses only the photos you select or take. They are stored on your device with your cards and are never uploaded to us.

Venue map link: You can add a map or location link for the venue so guests can find it. You enter or choose this link yourself. The App does not access your device's GPS or track your location.

Download and sharing: When you tap Download, the invitation card is saved to your device's storage or gallery. When you tap Share, the App uses Android's standard share menu to pass the card to an app you choose, such as WhatsApp, email or social media. We do not receive or store the cards you save or share, or who you share them with. Once shared, the card is handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy. Please share cards containing other people's names and photos only with their permission.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your invitation details and photos are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses camera access only if you choose to take a photo, the system photo picker or storage access to let you choose photos and save your cards, and internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not access your contacts, microphone or location. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

2. How information is used

  • Invitation details and photos are used only on your device to create, edit, display, save and share your cards as you request.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service, which collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If you open a venue map link, it opens in your map app or browser, such as Google Maps, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. Your invitation details, photos and cards never leave your device unless you choose to share them yourself. Advertising information is collected directly by Google AdMob as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your cards, drafts, details and photos remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by deleting them in the App, clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → Ahvanam → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Cards you downloaded to your gallery or storage are kept until you delete them, for example with your Gallery or Files app. Because we never receive your data, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
