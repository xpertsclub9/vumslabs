Privacy Policy for Smart Object Counter

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.smart_object_counter

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Smart Object Counter mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal information.

Your images stay on your device. The App lets you take a photo or use the live camera, or choose an image from your gallery, to count objects. Images are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent to us or any third party, and we cannot see them.

Object counting happens on your device. The App uses on-device machine learning (such as Google ML Kit or TensorFlow Lite models built into the App) to detect and count objects. Your images and results are not sent to any online service to do this. If ML Kit is used, it may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and feature performance or error metrics, to maintain the service. This information does not include your images.

Count history: Your past counts, including the counted images, results and dates, are saved only on your device so you can view them later. You can delete them at any time (see Section 5).

Download and sharing: When you tap Download, the counted image or result is saved to your device's storage or gallery. When you tap Share, the App uses Android's standard share menu to pass it to an app you choose, such as email, WhatsApp or a cloud drive. We do not receive or store what you save or share, or who you share it with. Once shared, it is handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your images and counts are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses camera access to capture images for counting, the system photo picker or storage access to let you choose images and save results, and internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not access your contacts, microphone or location. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

2. How information is used

  • Images are used only on your device to detect and count objects, and to show, save and share results as you request.
  • Count history is used only on your device so you can review past counts.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google ML Kit / TensorFlow Lite (on-device object detection): ML Kit Terms · Google Privacy Policy

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. Your images and count history never leave your device unless you choose to share them yourself. Advertising information is collected directly by Google AdMob as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Images and count history remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by deleting them in the App's history, clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → Smart Object Counter → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Images you downloaded to your gallery or storage are kept until you delete them, for example with your Gallery or Files app. Because we never receive your data, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
