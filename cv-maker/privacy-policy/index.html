Privacy Policy for CV Maker

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.cv_maker

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the CV Maker mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in.

Your CV details stay on your device. To build your CV, you may enter personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, date of birth, education, work experience, skills, references and other details you choose to include. This information is stored and processed only on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers or sent to us or any third party, and we cannot see it.

Profile photo: You can add a photo to your CV by choosing one from your gallery or, if you choose, taking one with your camera. The App accesses only the photo you select or take. It is stored on your device with your CV and is never uploaded to us.

PDF export and sharing: You can save your CV as a PDF file on your device. When you tap Share, the App uses Android's standard share menu to pass the PDF to an app you choose, such as email, WhatsApp or a cloud drive. We do not receive or store the files you save or share, or who you share them with. Once shared, the file is handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your CV details and photo are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses internet and network-state access to load ads. It may ask for camera access only if you choose to take a profile photo, and it uses the system photo picker or storage access only to let you choose a photo or save your PDF. It does not access your contacts, microphone or location. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

2. How information is used

  • The details and photo you enter are used only on your device to create, display, save and share your CV as you request.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service, which collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. Your CV details and photo never leave your device unless you choose to share the PDF yourself. Advertising information is collected and processed directly by Google AdMob as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your CV details and photo remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by deleting the CV in the App, clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → CV Maker → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. PDF files you saved to your device's storage are kept until you delete them, for example with your Files app. Because we never receive this information, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
