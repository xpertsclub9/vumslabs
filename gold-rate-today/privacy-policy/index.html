Privacy Policy for Gold Rate Today

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.gold_rate_today

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Gold Rate Today mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.

Gold price data from third-party providers: The App shows gold (and related metal) prices that it downloads from third-party market data providers through their APIs. To get the latest rates, the App sends a request to these providers that includes only what is needed to return prices, such as the selected city or market, currency and units. As with any internet request, the provider's servers also receive your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as app version). We do not send your name, contact details or any other personal information to these providers. The price data flows to your device. It is not information about you.

Your city and preferences stay on your device. You choose your city manually. The App does not use GPS or access your device's location. Your selected city, currency, units and other preferences are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Sharing rates: When you tap Share, the App uses Android's standard share menu to pass the gold rate text or image to an app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Messages. We do not receive or store what you share or who you share it with. Once shared, it is handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows banner and full-screen (interstitial) ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses only internet and network-state access, which it needs to download gold prices and load ads. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.

2. How information is used

  • Your selected city, currency and units are used to request and display the right gold rates.
  • Preferences are used only on your device to remember your choices.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Gold price data providers: third-party market data APIs that supply the prices shown in the App. They receive only the request details described in Section 1.
  • Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads): Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. The App sends only price requests (city or market, currency, units) to gold price data providers, and advertising information is collected directly by Google AdMob, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your preferences remain on your device until you change them, clear the App's data (Settings → Apps → Gold Rate Today → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App. Because we never receive your data, we hold no copy of it. Price data providers and Google retain request and advertising data according to their own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Disclaimer: Gold rates shown in the App are provided by third-party sources for general information only. They may be delayed, may differ from prices at your local jeweller and may not include taxes or making charges. The App does not provide financial or investment advice, and does not buy or sell gold.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy
