Privacy Policy for Langbell

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.langbell

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Langbell mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal information.

Learning progress stays on your device. Your lessons, progress, scores and preferences are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent to us, and we cannot see them.

Microphone and speech practice: If you use speaking or pronunciation exercises, the App asks for microphone access. Your voice is processed by your device's built-in Android speech recognition service (on most devices provided by Google) only to convert what you say into text so the App can check your answer. Depending on your device and settings, this service may process audio on your device or send it to Google to be recognized, under Google's Privacy Policy. The App does not record, save or upload your audio, and we never receive it. You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time and still use the rest of the App.

Text-to-speech: The App uses your device's built-in Android text-to-speech engine to read words and phrases aloud. Only lesson text is passed to it. No personal information is involved.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your learning progress and voice are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses internet and network-state access to load ads, and microphone access only for speaking exercises you choose to use. It does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera or location.

2. How information is used

  • Learning progress is used only on your device to track your lessons and show your results.
  • Speech input is used only to check your spoken answers and pronunciation, and is not kept.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Android speech recognition and text-to-speech: the speech services on your device, usually provided by Google (Google Privacy Policy)

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. Your learning progress never leaves your device. Speech is handled by your device's speech service as described above, and advertising information is collected directly by Google AdMob. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your learning progress remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → Langbell → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Your audio is not stored by the App. Because we never receive your information, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising and speech data according to its own policies, and you can manage this in your Google account and device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
