Privacy Policy for Passport Photo Maker

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.passport_photo_maker

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Passport Photo Maker mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal information.

Your photos stay on your device. The App lets you take a photo with your camera or choose one from your gallery, then crop, resize and edit it, including removing or changing the background, to create passport, visa and ID photos. Your photos and the finished passport photos are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent to us or any third party, and we cannot see them.

Background removal happens on your device. Background removal and editing are performed entirely on your device. Your photos are not sent to any online service to do this.

No facial recognition or biometric data. Your photos contain your face, but the App does not identify who you are, does not create face templates or other biometric identifiers, and does not collect or store biometric data.

Download and sharing: When you tap Download, the passport photo or print sheet is saved to your device's storage or gallery. When you tap Share, the App uses Android's standard share menu to pass the image to an app you choose, such as email, WhatsApp, a printing service or a cloud drive. We do not receive or store the images you save or share, or who you share them with. Once shared, the image is handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your photos are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses camera access to take photos, the system photo picker or storage access to let you choose photos and save your passport photos, and internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not access your contacts, microphone or location. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

2. How information is used

  • Photos are used only on your device to create, edit, display, save and share passport photos as you request.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service, which collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect your personal information, so we have none to share. Your photos never leave your device unless you choose to share them yourself. Advertising information is collected directly by Google AdMob as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Photos remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by deleting them in the App, clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → Passport Photo Maker → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Passport photos you downloaded to your gallery or storage are kept until you delete them, for example with your Gallery or Files app. Because we never receive your photos, we hold no copy of them. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Note: Passport Photo Maker is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government or passport authority. Please check your country's official photo requirements before submitting a photo.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
