Privacy Policy for AutoPay Guard

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.autopay_guard

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the AutoPay Guard mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, bank login, card number, UPI PIN or any other credentials.

SMS access: what we read and why

AutoPay Guard's core feature is finding and tracking the autopay mandates, subscriptions, recurring payments and debit alerts set up on your bank accounts, cards and UPI apps, so you can see what will be charged and when, and be reminded before money is debited. To do this, with your permission, the App reads SMS messages on your device (READ_SMS) and new incoming SMS (RECEIVE_SMS).

  • Only financial messages are used. The App looks only at transactional messages from banks, card issuers, UPI apps and payment services, such as autopay registrations, mandate alerts, debit and credit alerts and payment reminders. It ignores your personal conversations and all other messages, and does not store them.
  • What is extracted: from those financial messages, the App extracts only the details needed for the feature, such as the merchant or biller name, amount, date, frequency, the bank or UPI app name and the last few digits of the account or card shown in the message.
  • Processed only on your device. All SMS reading and analysis happens entirely on your device. SMS messages and the details extracted from them are never uploaded to our servers, never sent to us and never shared with any third party, including advertisers. We cannot see your messages.
  • Your choice. The App asks for SMS permission only after explaining this on screen, and only with your consent. You can deny or revoke SMS permission at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → AutoPay Guard → Permissions → SMS). Without it, automatic detection will not work.

Autopay records stay on your device. The autopays, subscriptions and reminders found by the App, or added by you, are stored only on your device.

Reminder notifications: With your permission, the App shows notifications to remind you before an autopay or recurring payment is due. Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain no data sent to or from us.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your SMS messages and financial details are never shared with AdMob or used for advertising. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses SMS read and receive access as described above, notification access for reminders, and internet and network-state access, which is used only to load ads. It does not access your contacts, call logs, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

2. How information is used

  • Financial SMS messages are used only on your device to detect and list your autopays, subscriptions and recurring payments.
  • Extracted payment details are used only on your device to show your autopay list and schedule reminders.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never sell, share, transfer or use SMS data for advertising, credit scoring, lending or any purpose other than the App's autopay tracking feature.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service, which collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not share any of your data. We do not collect your personal information, and your SMS messages, payment details and autopay records never leave your device. The only information that leaves your device is the advertising information collected directly by Google AdMob, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law, but because we never receive your SMS or financial data, we have none to disclose.

5. Data retention and deletion

The App does not keep copies of your SMS messages. It keeps only the autopay details it extracted, on your device, until you delete them. You can delete individual entries in the App, remove all App data by clearing it (Settings → Apps → AutoPay Guard → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App. Revoking SMS permission stops all further reading. Because we never receive your data, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Disclaimer: AutoPay Guard is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any bank, card network, NPCI or UPI app. It does not access your bank accounts, does not ask for your banking credentials and does not make payments. Detected information depends on the SMS you receive and may be incomplete. Always confirm details with your bank or payment app.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
