Privacy Policy for Smart Wakeup

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.smart_wakeup

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the Smart Wakeup mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

No account required. The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal information.

Your alarms stay on your device. The alarms you create, including times, repeat days, labels, sounds and settings, are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent to us.

Alarm background images: You can take a photo with your camera or choose one from your gallery to show as the background of your alarm screen. The App accesses only the image you take or select. It is saved only on your device, inside the App, and is never uploaded, shared with anyone or sent to us. We cannot see your images.

Information collected by advertising: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Your images and alarms are not shared with AdMob. See Section 3.

Permissions: The App uses the camera only if you choose to take a background photo, and the system photo picker or storage access only to let you choose an image. To make alarms work reliably, it uses permissions to schedule exact alarms, show notifications and a full-screen alarm, vibrate, keep the device awake while an alarm rings and restore your alarms after the device restarts. It also uses internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not access your contacts, microphone or location. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings, although some alarm features may then stop working.

2. How information is used

  • Alarm settings are used only on your device to ring your alarms at the times you choose.
  • Background images are used only on your device to display on your alarm screen.
  • Advertising information is used by Google AdMob to show ads (personalized or non-personalized), limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service, which collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Controlling personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not share any of your data. We do not collect your personal information, and your images and alarms never leave your device. The only information that leaves your device is the advertising information collected directly by Google AdMob, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your alarms and background images remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by deleting them in the App, clearing the App's data (Settings → Apps → Smart Wakeup → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Because we never receive your data, we hold no copy of it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit where data is transmitted, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
