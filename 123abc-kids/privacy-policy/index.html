Privacy Policy for 123abc Kids

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Package: com.vumslabs.abc_kids

This Privacy Policy explains how VUMS LABS, the app publishing brand of Procom Soft Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the 123abc Kids mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from children or anyone else. The App does not require an account or login, and it never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location or any other personal information.

Learning progress stays on the device. Stars, scores, levels and settings are stored only on the device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent to us.

Sounds and text-to-speech: The App plays letter, number and word sounds using built-in audio and the device's Android text-to-speech engine. Only lesson text is passed to it.

Microphone: saying letters aloud. In speaking activities, the App uses the device's built-in Android speech recognition service (on most devices provided by Google) to check whether the child said a letter, number or word correctly. Depending on the device and its settings, this service may process audio on the device or send it to Google to be recognized, under Google's Privacy Policy. The App does not keep this audio, and we never receive it.

Microphone: record and play back. In some activities, a child can record their own voice and play it back. These recordings are saved only on the device, are never uploaded or shared, and can be deleted at any time (see Section 5). We never receive them.

Microphone access is requested only when a speaking or recording activity is opened. A parent can deny or revoke it at any time in the device settings, and the rest of the App will still work.

Ads: The App shows ads through Google AdMob configured for child-directed treatment (in line with COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy). This means only non-personalized, family-appropriate ads are shown. No interest-based advertising or remarketing is used, and the advertising ID is not used for personalization. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may still process limited technical information such as IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device type and operating system, as permitted for child-directed services. This information is processed by Google, not by us. Recordings and progress are never shared with AdMob.

Permissions: The App uses the microphone only for speaking and recording activities, and internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not access contacts, the camera, photos, files or location.

2. How information is used

  • Progress is used only on the device to track learning and show rewards.
  • Speech input is used only to check spoken answers, and is not kept.
  • Voice recordings are used only on the device so the child can play them back.
  • Limited technical information is used by Google AdMob only to serve contextual, non-personalized ads and to prevent fraud.
  • If a parent emails us, we use the email address and message only to respond.

We do not sell any information, and we do not use any information to build profiles of children.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (child-directed, non-personalized ads): Google Privacy Policy · AdMob and child-directed treatment
  • Android speech recognition and text-to-speech: the speech services on the device, usually provided by Google (Google Privacy Policy)

The App does not use analytics, social media features, chat, external links that lead children out of the App without a parental check, or any other service that collects personal information.

4. Data sharing

We do not collect personal information, so we have none to share. Progress and recordings never leave the device. Speech is handled by the device's speech service, and limited technical information is processed by Google AdMob, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Progress and voice recordings remain on the device until they are deleted. A parent can delete recordings in the App, or remove all App data by clearing it (Settings → Apps → 123abc Kids → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the App. Speech audio is not stored by the App. Because we never receive any of this information, we hold no copy of it. For any privacy question or request, email playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Because data stays on the device, it is protected by the device's own security. Any communication with ad services uses encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

123abc Kids is designed for children and families and complies with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR (including its provisions for children) and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not ask children for personal information, does not offer chat or social features, and shows only non-personalized, family-appropriate ads.

Parents and guardians: You can review or delete everything the App stores on the device at any time, as described in Section 5. If you believe any personal information about your child has been collected, contact us at playstore@procomsoftsol.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
